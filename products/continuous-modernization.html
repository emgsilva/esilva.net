Why now: AI accelerates the rate of change

New technology, market demands and expectations change faster every year, while most organizations' operating models change much more slowly. As that gap grows, costs go up and sustainability goes down. AI widens that gap further: it amplifies whatever your organization already is, and if your sociotechnical architecture does not evolve with it, AI accelerates sociotechnical debt and creates more problems than it solves. Keeping up means moving towards continuous improvement, continuously developing your capabilities: close existing gaps with enabling activities, then scale sustainably through platforms.

What you will learn

In two steps, applied to your own organization.

01

Get modernization moving

Find the business opportunities and capability gaps that matter, and close them with an Architecture Modernization Enabling Team (AMET) and other practices that build early momentum. The same approach works for other big gaps, such as adopting AI or removing any other blocker that holds your business back.

02

Make it continuous

Build modernization capabilities into the organization itself, so it keeps up as change accelerates: for example ADRs, RFCs, the advice process, and platforms that let practices like AI scale sustainably across teams. Teams then modernize locally, experts help where needed, and a big AMET is no longer required.

What you leave with

  • A clear view of the business opportunities and capability gaps that matter most in your organization
  • A plan to close your most important gap, with an AMET or another enabling activity
  • The first practices to make modernization continuous: ADRs, RFCs, the advice process, and a platform approach to scale sustainably
  • A personal follow-up plan, shaped in your one-to-one
  • A group of peers working through the same challenges, and a follow-up session to check progress
