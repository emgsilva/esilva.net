What you will learn

You learn the principles and practices in two steps. Throughout, you work on your own organization's challenges and start shaping ideas to bring into your own context.

01

Get modernization moving

How to navigate the first steps of a modernization: understanding the business opportunities that matter and the capability gaps to close, when and how to set up an Architecture Modernization Enabling Team (AMET), and the other practices that build early momentum.

02

Make it continuous

How to build modernization capabilities into the organization itself, such as ADRs, RFCs and the advice process. Teams can then modernize locally, experts help where they are needed, and a big AMET is no longer required. This takes time and effort, and you learn how to develop it.

How it runs

Live online for two months, in alternating weeks. One week, a session of 2 to 3 hours. The next, you practice on your own case, with office hours for your specific questions. A group of 15 people at most.

Who it is for

Product and technology leaders, architects, engineering and product managers, coaches and transformation agents.
