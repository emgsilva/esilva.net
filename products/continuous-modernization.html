Why now: AI raises the stakes

AI amplifies whatever your organization already is. On top of tangled systems, unclear ownership and slow decisions, it produces more change, faster, into the same bottlenecks. Adopted and scaled without care, AI creates more problems than it solves: more complexity, more dependencies, more legacy, faster.

For most organizations, getting lasting value from AI is a modernization challenge: close existing capability gaps with enabling teams, then scale sustainably through platforms (two key ideas from Team Topologies), so every team can leverage those shared and consolidated capabilities without dependencies on experts, and AI leads to better outcomes, not more problems.

What you will learn

You learn the principles and practices in two steps. Throughout, you work on your own organization's challenges and start shaping ideas to bring into your own context.

01

Get modernization moving

How to navigate the first steps of a modernization: understanding the business opportunities that matter and the capability gaps to close, when and how to set up an Architecture Modernization Enabling Team (AMET), and the other practices that build early momentum. The same enabling approach helps close other big gaps, such as adopting AI, moving to new platforms, or removing any other blocker that holds your business back.

02

Make it continuous

How to build modernization capabilities into the organization itself, such as ADRs, RFCs and the advice process, and platforms that let practices like AI scale sustainably across teams. Teams can then modernize locally, experts help where they are needed, and a big AMET is no longer required. This takes time and effort, and you learn how to develop it.
