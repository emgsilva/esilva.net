Why now: AI raises the stakes

AI amplifies whatever your organization already is. Adopted and scaled without care, it creates more problems than it solves: more complexity, more dependencies, more legacy, faster. For most organizations, getting lasting value from AI is a modernization challenge: close existing capability gaps with enabling teams, then scale sustainably through platforms (two key ideas from Team Topologies).

What you will learn

In two steps, applied to your own organization.

01

Get modernization moving

Find the business opportunities and capability gaps that matter, and close them with an Architecture Modernization Enabling Team (AMET) and other practices that build early momentum. The same approach works for other big gaps, such as adopting AI or removing any other blocker that holds your business back.

02

Make it continuous

Build modernization capabilities into the organization itself, such as ADRs, RFCs, the advice process, and platforms that let practices like AI scale sustainably across teams. Teams then modernize locally, experts help where needed, and a big AMET is no longer required.
